🚴‍♂️ AI Performance Coach • Elite Suite | User Guide
Welcome to your AI Performance Coach—a cutting-edge, personalized training companion that bridges the analytical power of Intervals.icu, the physiological guardrails of an elite sports scientist, and the conversational adaptability of an AI coach.

🔐 1. Getting Started & Authentication
When you first launch the app, you will be greeted by the Elite Athlete Portal:

Owner Login: If you have an established account linked to your Supabase cloud backend, log in securely with your credentials.

Friend / Guest Setup: If you are setting up a guest session, enter your Name, your Intervals.icu Athlete ID, and your Intervals.icu API Key. The app will securely cache your configuration locally in your browser storage so you won't need to re-enter it.

☀️ 2. Navigation & Core Pages
The app is organized into five powerful modules accessible via your sidebar navigation:

📊 A. Command Center
Physiological Readiness Card: The top banner instantly grades your daily readiness using a color-coded status card (Green for ready, Red/Crimson if the Readiness Gatekeeper has tripped due to high fatigue or poor sleep).

Metrics & PMC Chart: View your core training metrics at a glance—Fitness (CTL), Fatigue (ATL), and Form (TSB)—backed by a 90-day interactive Performance Management Chart.

90-Day Trend Synthesis: Click Run 90-Day Trend Synthesis to have the AI analyze your last 3 months of training load, saving your last 3 reports for easy review or discussion.

🤖 B. AI Coach & Sparring
Conversational Sparring: Chat live with your AI coach. Choose from three personas in your sidebar (Collaborative Peer, Sports Scientist, or Drill Sergeant).

The Readiness Gatekeeper in Action: If your Form (TSB) drops below -20 or your sleep score tanks below 60, the AI is hardcoded to veto high-intensity requests and mandate recovery.

Instant Workout Approvals: When the coach proposes a weekly training block, a structured action card appears below the chat with an "Approve & Sync" button that pushes the ERG-ready workout straight to Intervals.icu and MyWhoosh.

📅 C. Training Calendar
Unified Schedule: View your past 14 days of completed rides and next 14 days of planned workouts, grouped by date.

Manual Workout Pusher: Use the expandable form to manually build and push custom workouts (complete with exact power percentages and target cadence in RPM) directly to your calendar.

🔍 D. Activity Inspector
Deep-Dive Debriefs: Select any recently completed ride from your Intervals.icu sync history.

Execution Compliance Score: The app automatically calculates a deterministic Variability Index (VI) score to grade how smoothly you executed your target power without excessive surging.

AI Debriefs: Hit Run AI Debrief to get an instant performance breakdown tailored to your race goals.

🗺️ Route Strategist
GPX Course Analysis: Upload any .gpx route file to instantly calculate total distance, elevation gain, and max elevation via stylized metric cards.

Race-Day Fueling Calculator: Adjust your estimated completion time to instantly calculate your precise hourly targets for Carbohydrates (grams), Fluids (liters), and Sodium (mg).

🧠 3. Advanced Features
Coach's Long-Term Memory Notebook: Located in the sidebar. This running notebook records your unique physiological quirks, injuries, and training preferences. Even if you clear your chat history, the AI reads this notebook on every prompt so it never forgets your style.

Gear & Supplement Tracker: Keep your bike build specifications (e.g., Cervélo Soloist, 160mm Magene cranks, custom gear ratios) and fueling supplements logged so the AI always factors your physical setup into its advice.
